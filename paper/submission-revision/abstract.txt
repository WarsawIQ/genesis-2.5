GENESIS is a long-established compartmental neural simulator. GENESIS 2.5 adds optional CUDA and OpenCL backends to its hsolve solver, including a GPU Hines elimination for dendritic-tree populations, without changing existing models, scripts or MPI workflows. On 160 000 compartments over 10 000 steps an A100 runs 24.0x faster end to end than the CPU solver; in double precision it reproduces the CPU solver to every printed digit. Networks that exchange spikes every step gain nothing on the GPU, and we show why. The release also makes model construction linear and fixes two defects in the Hines solver of GENESIS 2.4.
