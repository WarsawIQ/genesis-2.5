GENESIS is a long-established compartmental neural simulator. GENESIS 2.5 adds optional CUDA and OpenCL backends to its hsolve solver, including a GPU Hines elimination for dendritic-tree populations, without changes to existing models or scripts. On 160 000 compartments over 10 000 steps an A100 runs 24.0x faster end to end than the CPU solver. In double precision it matches the CPU solver to ten significant figures. Spiking networks with zero axonal delay run slower, because the host delivers spikes every step. The release also makes model construction linear in the common case and fixes three Hines-solver defects of GENESIS 2.4.
