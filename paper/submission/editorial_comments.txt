This is a resubmission of SOFTX-D-26-00952, rejected in August 2026.

Reviewer 2 asked for a direct comparison against contemporary GPU-enabled
simulators, on published workloads, under one benchmarking methodology. We
carried out that work rather than argue the point: the manuscript now compares
GENESIS 2.5 with NEURON, CoreNEURON and Arbor, including their GPU backends,
on the published Vogels-Abbott COBAHH benchmark and on a 160,000-compartment
multi-compartment population, with a single end-to-end timing definition
throughout. A point-by-point response to both reviewers is attached.

Two procedural notes:

1. The form asks for a Code Ocean capsule. We have not made one. The paper's
central claim is GPU acceleration, and a capsule without an NVIDIA card would
run only the CPU paths. We instead provide the GitHub repository, an archived
release (10.5281/zenodo.22032886) of the exact version measured, and a
reproduction pack that re-measures the claims on the reviewer's own hardware.
We are glad to prepare a capsule for the CPU-only claims if you would like one.

2. The software is at https://github.com/WarsawIQ/genesis-2.5 with README.md
and a licence file, as the Guide for Authors requires.
